# Hi, I'm Valéria Vieira dos Santos

### Computational Linguist | AI Evaluation & Conversational AI Specialist

I design evaluation frameworks for multilingual conversational AI, combining computational linguistics, statistical analysis, and six years of experience in AI products and customer-experience programs.

My current PhD research investigates whether language-model confidence reflects pragmatic uncertainty in spontaneous Brazilian Portuguese. I am particularly interested in human evaluation, annotation quality, conversational reliability, and translating research findings into measurable product criteria.

## Selected work

### [Speech Disfluencies and LLM Confidence](https://github.com/ValeriaVSantos/uncertainty-signature-audit)

An audit of model confidence across faithful and sanitized Roda Viva transcripts. Published at CODI-CRAC 2026. The study finds that turn length dominates pragmatic uncertainty markers as a predictor of model confidence.

### [Political Evasion Detection in Brazilian Portuguese](https://github.com/ValeriaVSantos/roda-viva-evasion)

A three-class benchmark for direct, partial, and evasive responses in political interviews. The current extension includes partially overlapping annotations from three human annotators and reproducible agreement analysis.
